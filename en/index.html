PokeAndScan Privacy Policy

Published by FalconSocka. PokeAndScan is an independent Android companion. It is not an official Pokémon GO or Niantic service and is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.

Last updated: 7 October 2026

English Slovenčina

This policy describes how the current PokeAndScan Android app handles information. You choose when to start a live scan or select an MP4 recording for processing. Processing happens locally on your Android device, subject to the supported Android version and recording profile. Scan data stays in app-managed local storage. The current app includes scanning, review, and export features; no PokeAndScan account or server is required.

On-device data and processing

Recognition and review happen on your device and offline. PokeAndScan does not use cloud OCR or upload videos, screen frames, scan results, or evidence images. The current app has no analytics, advertising, or tracking service and does not use a third-party service to collect app data.

The app stores your preferences; scan and snapshot names, scope descriptions, and timestamps; recognized or manually entered Pokémon fields; unresolved review items; original parser observations and your current corrections; and small evidence crops needed to review results. Intermediate edit steps are not kept as a full history. A clean record keeps a minimal representative appraisal crop. Uncertain, partial, corrected, or nickname-related fields may keep relevant field crops. A missed appraisal event may keep a transition crop. The full source recording is not copied or retained by default.

Screen capture and selected recordings

Live capture starts only after you choose it and grant Android's screen-capture consent. While a live scan is active, frames provided by Android may include content visible on the captured screen, including another app or a system screen. PokeAndScan checks frames in memory to identify relevant game screens. It discards non-game frames and never stores them as images or evidence. If you leave Pokémon GO, PokeAndScan may retain only a warning type and source time for those frames; the scan does not stop automatically. PokeAndScan does not control Pokémon GO, tap or swipe for you, or capture microphone or audio samples. On Android versions that require it, notification permission is requested when you start a live scan, not during setup. A foreground-service notification shows ongoing work and provides a stop action where Android makes it available. Notification permission is not requested solely for MP4 processing.

For an MP4 scan, you choose a recording using Android's file picker or share flow. The app processes its content URI without copying the whole video by default. It keeps read access while processing or while an unfinished scan may need recovery, then releases that access when the scan is completed or discarded. Audio-track samples are not decoded or used. Releasing access does not delete or modify your original recording.

Storage, backup, and deletion

Preferences, scan results, review data, and evidence are stored in app-managed local storage on your device. They remain there until you delete the scan or clear PokeAndScan's app data in Android Settings. Deleting a scan removes its app-owned records, review data, evidence, and processing metadata, but does not delete a source MP4 stored outside the app. The app does not provide a delete-all-data command; to clear all app data, use Android Settings.

Android Auto Backup is disabled for PokeAndScan. Backup rules also exclude app data from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Some Android manufacturers may not honor device-to-device transfer exclusions.

Exports

CSV and JSON exports are created only when you ask for them. When you share an export with another app, PokeAndScan stages a copy in its private cache. When you create a later shared export, PokeAndScan removes shared-export cache files older than seven days; Android may clear the cache sooner. If you do not share another export, an older cache file may remain until Android clears it or a later share triggers cleanup. Deleting a scan does not remove an export already created. You can save or share an export outside app-managed storage; the receiving app or destination may retain its own copy, which PokeAndScan cannot control or delete. Exports are not a full backup of snapshots, evidence, or review data.
